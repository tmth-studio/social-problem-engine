# Verification boundary

The verifier is intentionally narrower than the architecture method.

It checks structure, traceability, provenance declarations, financial arithmetic and a published margin-of-safety threshold. For the problem-first stages it checks that each stage is present, complete and traced to evidence records that exist. It cannot establish that the causal chain is true or that the intervention point is well chosen. It cannot establish that a customer will buy, or that the venture will move the outcome metric by the amount stated. It cannot establish that a cited source is true or that an expert endorses the result.

The generator may propose architecture. It may not alter `rules/policy.json`, the verifier, score thresholds, or a report for its own artifact. Changes to those files require an independent review and a version bump.

## Qualification status

This project is not evidence that Forge WS1 is achieved, and it is not evidence that any social problem has been or will be fixed. Use the term **mechanically verified against v0.2** only.
